Questions

Frequently Asked

Are you licensed for solar installation?

Yes — our electricians hold Clean Energy Council accreditation for solar PV and battery storage installation.

Do you offer emergency call-outs?

Yes, 24/7 for genuine electrical emergencies such as sparking, burning smells or total outages.

Will I get a compliance certificate?

Every job that requires one includes a Certificate of Electrical Safety at completion, at no extra cost.

How much notice do you need?

Standard call-outs are usually available same-day or next-day. Larger jobs like rewiring are scheduled 1-2 weeks out.

Do you work on older homes?

Yes — a large part of our work is bringing older wiring up to current safety standards without unnecessary demolition.

Need an electrician you can rely on?

Same-day call-outs, upfront pricing, compliance guaranteed.
