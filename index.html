Frequently Asked

Are you licensed for solar installation?

Yes — our electricians hold Clean Energy Council accreditation for solar PV and battery storage.

Do you offer emergency call-outs?

Yes, 24/7 for genuine electrical emergencies such as sparking, burning smells or total outages.

Will I get a compliance certificate?

Every job that requires one includes a Certificate of Electrical Safety at completion, at no extra cost.

How much notice do you need?

Standard call-outs are usually available same-day or next-day. Larger jobs are scheduled 1-2 weeks out.
